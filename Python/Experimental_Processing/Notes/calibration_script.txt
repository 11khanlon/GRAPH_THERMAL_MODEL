What is homography? 

* Homography is a projective transformation between two planes 
* Homography matrix is what connects the camera coordinates (u, v) to real coordinates (x, y) 

Your camera is viewing a flat surface from some angle:
camera image [u, v]:
       1────────────2
      /              \
     /                \
    4──────────────────3


But physically, you know that substrate is a rectangle: 
physical world [x,y]:
(0,W) ───────── (L,W)
  2                 3
  │                 │
  │                 │
  1─────────────────4
(0,0)             (L,0)


The physical coordinates could be flat, but the image looks slanted (like a parallelogram)
A homography transforms coordinates on one flat plane into coordiantes in another view 
[u', v', w']^T = H[x, y, 1]^T 
u = u'/w', v = v'/w' 
where: 
- x,y = real plate coordinate 
- u,v = camera pixel 
- H = 3x3 transformation matrix 

p_hom = [float(x), float(y), 1]
p1_hom = H @ p_hom


To find H matrix:
Use the known physical substrate coordinates [x,y]
Manually identify camera pixels [u,v]
Solve for the H matrix
