FLIR calibration constant:
the camera exposes radiometric calibration constants 
They're used to convert raw detector signal into radiance and ultimately temperature 

Convert taw image singal into pseudo-radiance: 
J1 = gain 
J0 = Offset 

image_Radiance = (image_data - J0) / J1

Emissivity: controls how much of the observed radiation is attributed to the actual emitting surface 
TRefl = reflected apparent temperature 
The camera doesn't only see radiation emitted by your target, it also sees reflected environmental radiation 
For metallic DED surface, reflection can matter a lot because emissivity can be low 
atmospheric temperature: 293.15 
humidity = 0.55 --> this means 55% relative humidity. It's used because water vapor absorbs infared radiation
