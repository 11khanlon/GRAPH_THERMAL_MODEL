Overall purpose: Main deposition simulation 
The paper solves the process in this order 

1. deposit material 
2. Heat it with the laser 
3. Conduct heat 
4. Lose heat through convection 
5. Move to next block 
6. After entire layer allow dwell cooling 
7. Repeat 

Implements Section 4.3.1

Simulation order: 

for every block
--> activate nodes 
--> apply Goldak heat source 
--> conduction 
--> convection 
and block 

after layer: 
--> dwell conduction 
--> dwell convection 
repeat until final layer
