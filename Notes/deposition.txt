What happends physically when the laser is moving? 
The laser deposits energy into the material. At essentially the same time:
--> heat conducts away from the hot region 
--> heat is lost from the exposed surfaces through convection 
--> Radiation also removes heat 
--> The surrounding material is heated 
--> Repat by inputting temperature state into laser model

So physically, there isn't a sqeuence like:
Laser heating, wait, conduciton, wait, convection 
Instead, these mechanisms happen simultaneously 

This paper uses a sequential numerical approximation:
laser heating --> conduction --> convection/radiation calculation --> next laser position 
For example: 
T0 --> Laser B1 --> conduction --> convection --> T1 
T1 --> Laser B2 --> Conduction --> convection --> T2 
Not solving as one coupled equation

Convection:
qconv = hA(T - Tinfinity)
T(t) = Tinifinity + (T0 - Tinifinity)exp(-beta*t) 


Conduction changes the temperature distribution, and that temperature distribution determines how much 
heat can subsequently be removed form exposed surfaces
