Biot number review 

Bi = hLc/k 
h = surface heat-transfer [W / m^2K]
Lc = characteristic length [m]
k = solid thermal conductivity [W/mK]

Bi can be interpreted as: 
Bi ~ internal conduction resistance / surface heat transfer resistance 
Rcond ~ Lc / kA 
Rconv ~ 1 / hA 
Rcond/Rconv ~ hLc/k = Bi 

If Bi < 0.1 
This means that the resistance in conduction is less than the resistance in the surface layer heat transfer 

Internal solid                    Surface
conduction                        heat transfer

Rcond = SMALL                     Rconv = LARGE
       │                                 │
       ▼                                 ▼
 ┌──────────────┐                  ┌─────────┐
 │      SOLID   │ ───────────────► │ SURFACE │ ──► gas
 └──────────────┘                  └─────────┘

The solid can internally conduct heat easily compared to the rate that heat can leave the surface 
since the resitiance for convection is larger (heat has more trouble leaving)

What is a lumped body? 
A lumped bpdy is a body whose internal temperature gradients are small enough to approximate 
T(x, y, z, t)  = T(t). This is lumped capacitance where you lump the entire temperature field 
The DED graph model is not a lumped capacitance model of the enitre substrate or entire part 
The graph conduction solver calculates different temperatures at different nodes 
The model is designed to capture spatial temperature gradients 

The model lumps several surface heat-loss mechanisms
Lump several surface heat-loss mechanisms: qconv + qrad 

Note: 
The characteristic length of a lumped cooling model is Lc = V/ As
The use block length which is much larger than Lc
more of a worst case scenario. Otherwise, the Beta term would be huge
